About us

About NaijaHub UK Ltd

A UK-based company bringing technology, administration, office support and residential property management together, with one point of contact for all four.

Our company

Who we are

NaijaHub UK Ltd is a UK-based private limited company offering four complementary services: software development and IT solutions, document preparation and administrative support, office support services, and residential property management.

We bring these services together so that businesses, landlords and individuals can get practical help without having to coordinate several different providers. Whether you need a new piece of software, help with paperwork, extra support in the office or someone to look after a property, we aim to make the process clear and straightforward.

“

Our mission

To make technology, administration and property management simpler for the people we work with, through practical support, clear communication and work done with care.

Our values

The principles behind our work

Five values guide how we work with you, whichever service you choose.

Integrity

We are honest about what we can and cannot do, and open about how we work.

Reliability

We do what we say we will do, and we let you know if anything changes.

Clarity

We explain things in plain English, so you know what is happening and what comes next.

Care

We treat your business, your documents and your property with the care we would give our own.

Quality

We take pride in accurate, well-presented work and pay close attention to detail.

How we work

Our approach

Good support starts with listening. Before recommending anything, we take the time to understand your situation, your priorities and what a good outcome looks like for you. We then agree a clear way forward and keep you informed as the work progresses.

  1. 01

    Understand first

    We ask questions and listen carefully, so our work is based on what you actually need.

  2. 02

    Keep it practical

    We favour sensible, straightforward solutions that are easy to use and easy to maintain.

  3. 03

    Communicate clearly

    We explain things plainly and keep you updated, so you know what to expect.
