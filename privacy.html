Your privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This notice explains how NaijaHub UK Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects and uses personal information when you visit this website, naijahubukltd.shop, or contact us through it. We handle personal information in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

NaijaHub UK Ltd is the controller responsible for your personal information. We are a private limited company registered in [Place of registration], company number [Company number]. Our registered office is at [Registered office address].

If you have any questions about this notice or how we use your information, please email us at [Email address].

The information we collect

When you send us an enquiry using the contact form, we collect:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your phone number, if you choose to give it
  • the service you are interested in, if you select one
  • the content of your message

Please do not include sensitive information, such as details about your health, in your message. If you contact us by email or phone, we collect the information you choose to share with us.

How we use your information

We use your information to read and respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to discuss the services you have asked about. We do not sell your information, and we do not use the details you send through the contact form for marketing.

Our lawful basis

Under UK GDPR, we rely on:

  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): responding to enquiries that people send us.
  • Steps before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b)): where your enquiry relates to services you may want us to provide.

Who we share it with

We use the following service providers to run this website:

  • Web3Forms receives the information you enter in the contact form and delivers it to us by email.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts used on this website. To deliver them, your browser sends your IP address to Google when you visit.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub processes technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver the website and keep it secure.

We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as we need it to respond to your enquiry and deal with any follow-up. If you go on to use our services, we keep relevant information for as long as necessary to provide them and to meet our legal obligations, and then delete it securely.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • ask us to delete your information
  • ask us to restrict how we use your information
  • object to us using your information
  • ask us to transfer your information to another organisation, where this applies

To make a request, please email [Email address]. We may need to confirm your identity before we can act on it.

Making a complaint

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put things right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page, with the date it was last updated shown at the top.
